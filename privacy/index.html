Privacy policy

Vireo is a personal health and fitness companion. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated 23 October 2026

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and chosen username. Your password is never stored directly; only a secure cryptographic hash of it is kept.
  • Health and fitness data, read through Android Health Connect with your permission: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, recovery, activity and steps, calories, nutrition and food entries, hydration, weight, body fat, height and related metrics.
  • Data from your Google account, only if you connect it (see “Data from Google” below).
  • Profile and biometrics you enter: age, gender, height, weight, body-fat estimate and activity level.
  • Training logs you create, such as climbing sessions and finger-health notes.
  • App preferences, in-app notifications and an optional profile photo.

Data from Google

You can connect Vireo to parts of your Google account. Each connection is optional, asks for your permission on Google’s own screen, and can be removed at any time.

  • Fitbit data (Google Health API), read-only: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements (such as heart rate, heart rate variability and weight), sleep, your Fitbit profile and settings. Vireo uses this data to show your dashboard and history and to calculate your readiness, sleep and training load. Vireo never writes anything to your Fitbit account.
  • Google Calendar: Vireo lists your calendars so you can pick one, then adds and updates your planned training sessions in it. It only reads back the events it created itself.
  • Google Drive: Vireo can back up your climbing videos to your Drive. It can only see and change files it created; it cannot read anything else in your Drive.

Data from Google is stored on your device and in your personal Vireo account so it can follow you across devices. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described under “Third-party services”. If you agree to let Vee use your health data (see below), a summary that can include values that came from Fitbit is sent with your messages to Vee.

Vireo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Vireo from your Google account at any time in the app’s settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How we use your information

Your data is used to calculate your Capacity, sleep score and load, to show your dashboard and history, to generate your training plan, and to keep your information in sync across your devices.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored on your device and in your personal account in our cloud database, linked to your account identifier so it can follow you across devices.

Third-party services

  • Android Health Connect provides on-device access to your health data.
  • Google’s Gemini API reads text and images you choose to send, so the app can understand them: food you describe in words, photos of meals and nutrition labels, the optional free-text plan notes from training setup (off by default; you confirm the result before anything is applied), and your conversations with Vee. With your agreement, Vee’s messages also carry a summary of your recent data: Capacity, sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, today’s check-in, recent sessions, your training plan and the injury notes in your profile. You can withdraw that agreement in Settings; Vee then answers without your health data.
  • Supabase hosts the cloud database that stores your account data.
  • Open Food Facts is used to look up foods you scan by barcode and to search foods by name. The words you search for, and the food names from meals you log with Vee, are sent to it, together with your country and the app’s language, so it can show products sold there. No account details, name or health data are sent.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, revoke Health Connect permissions in your Android settings, disconnect your Google account as described above, and ask for your account data to be deleted by emailing us.

Children

Vireo is not intended for children under 12.

Changes
