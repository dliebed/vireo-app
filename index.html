Training and recovery for climbers.

Vireo plans your training, logs your sessions and reads your sleep and heart rate, so you can see how ready you are to climb. For iPhone and Android.

Vireo is in private testing and not yet in the App Store or Google Play.

A plan built around you

Vireo builds a climbing training plan from your grade, your goals and the days you can train.

Every session logged

Log boulders, hangboard sets and finger-health notes, and see how your training adds up.

Know when to push

Your sleep, heart rate and activity become one readiness score, so you know how hard to train today.

Connected only if you want it

Each connection is optional and asks for your permission first.
