Delete your account

How to delete your account in Vireo, the training app by Vireo Training, and what happens to your data.

Last updated 12 November 2026

In the app

Tap your profile picture at the top of the screen, open Account, then tap Delete account and confirm. Your data is removed from the cloud first, then from your phone, and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email privacy@vireotrain.app from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account and everything stored with it in our cloud database: your profile, training plans and logs, health and fitness data synced from your phone or your Google account, food and hydration entries, settings and profile photo.

What is not deleted

Data that lives outside Vireo stays where it is: your Health Connect data on your phone, your Fitbit data in your Google account, calendar events Vireo added to your Google Calendar, and videos it backed up to your Google Drive. You can remove those in Health Connect and your Google account. Crash reports you chose to send are kept by Google Firebase for up to 90 days and contain no account details.
